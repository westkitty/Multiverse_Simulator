## Biggest transformation: inhabitants are not a counter

`expedition.rest` consumes up to two **local** supplies per action, restoring twenty total actor energy per supply, at most forty total energy. It acts only at a visited, operational refuge on teams physically present in that region. Distribution is deterministic by actor ID. Fractional supplies pay only for actual remaining energy capacity.

No free expedition stock, remote healing, arbitrary work-counter increment or regenerated crew is involved. Empty, fully rested, broken, remote, in-transit and depleted refuges reject before changing stocks/actors; duplicate team identities anywhere in the roster also reject. The UI previews the same calculation, labels the action **Rest local teams**, shows real actor energy meters, and disables the action when no tired local teams remain. The causal event records recipients, before/after energies and cost in History & branches.

Observed production-browser journey, Wild Meridian at tick 5:

- Before: actor-0 = 78, actor-4 = 78.
- After one service: actor-0 = 100, actor-4 = 96.
- Cost: 2 local supplies; restored energy: 40 total.
- Expedition unchanged: fuel 149, supplies 79; tick and position unchanged.
- A second service fills the remaining capacity and disables the no-op action.

See `journey-desktop.json`, `journey-mobile.json` and the paired service screenshots. This is a modest, concrete increase in consequence and inhabitedness—not a claim to have achieved overwhelming awe throughout the project.

## Route guidance and information discipline

The briefing checks discovery, transit, destination hazard, blocked routes and fuel. Arrival/cost reflect the engine's committed route, not a safety forecast. The native environment still evolves during transit.

The optional planner only considers **revealed**, unresolved, prerequisite-ready destinations. It prioritizes short travel, then higher current hazard. Already-local work is shown as requiring no travel, even when the surroundings have deteriorated. It neither reveals unknown history nor moves the expedition. It is a greedy aid, not an optimal or all-knowing itinerary.

The previous 45-run full-kernel, default-stock test was rerun with the new plugins and passed. It remains a feasibility/stability workload, not proof of sustainable unattended economies or proof that this restricted UI reproduces its omniscient test policy.
