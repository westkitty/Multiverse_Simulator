## Gate Rules
- **No Pilot Worlds**: No feature may be marked complete while any of the 15 universes retains stub or placeholder behavior.
- **Proof Tiering**: Unit/mock tests (Tier 2) cannot certify live runtime behavior (Tier 4).
- **Synchronized Release**: Features are released across all 15 worlds together.
